5- **putting together our own gaming news service by parsing various RSS feeds**:

The incident with `GameSpot` news API clearly showed that third party news APIs are not dependable.
The best idea that I have achieved in this matter (with the help of AI) was to read various RSS feeds of famous gaming websites (**IGN**, **PC Gamer**, **Eurogamer**, and **Kotaku**) and then merge them together as our own news API. Thus reducing the risk of losing our news services if a specific 3rd party API goes down.
